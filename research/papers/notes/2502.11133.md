## Setup
- Tasks / benchmarks: MMLU (multitask reasoning), GSM8K (grade-school math), MATH (stratified 519 competition math problems), HumanEval (code generation), MBPP (Python programming); Section 5.1, p. 6.
- Models used: Heterogeneous candidate pool: gpt-4o-mini-0718, claude-3.5-haiku, gemini-1.5-flash, llama-3.1-70b; deepseek-v3 tested for inductive zero-shot generalization; Section 5.1, p. 6–7.
- Baselines: (1) Vanilla models (gpt-3.5-turbo up to llama-3.1-70b); (2) Single-agent CoT, ComplexCoT, Self-Consistency; (3) Fixed homogeneous MAS: Chain, Tree, Complete Graph (MacNet), LLM-Debate; (4) Dynamic MAS: GPTSwarm, AgentPrune, AFlow; (5) Single-agent routers: PromptLLM, RouteLLM, FrugalGPT, RouterDC; Table 1, p. 6.

## Topology tested
- Depth: Depth-1 to Depth-2 depending on the dynamically selected collaboration pattern (Chain, Tree, Star/Hub-and-Spoke, LLM-Debate, Complete Graph; Section 3.1 & 4.1, p. 3–5).
- Width: Dynamically determined per query via complexity mapping $k = \lceil \delta(H) \cdot \gamma \rceil$, where $\gamma$ is capped at 6 (varied 2 to 10 in sensitivity ablation; Section 4.1 & 5.5, p. 5, 8).
- Roles: 26 specialized functional roles (analyst, programmer, test engineer, physicist, mathematician, etc.; Section 5.1 & Appendix E).
- Communication / handoff format; who can write code/files: Cascaded controller network $F_\theta = F_\theta^m \circ F_\theta^r \circ F_\theta^t$:
  1. $F_\theta^t$: Variational latent model routes query to collaboration mode.
  2. $F_\theta^r$: Probabilistic cascade assigns sequential roles based on preceding role history.
  3. $F_\theta^m$: Multinomial router binds each allocated role to an optimal LLM backbone. Code/output produced by assigned worker roles in sequence or graph structure; Section 4.1–4.3, p. 4–5.

## Findings (quote numbers with table/figure/page refs)
- Depth: Structured multi-agent flows consistently outperform flat single-agent baselines across all tasks; however, static unrouted MAS incurs excessive token overhead (e.g., LLM-Debate without routing achieves 79.67% average score vs single-agent RouteLLM at 80.44% due to noisy inter-agent debate; Table 1, p. 6).
- Width: Sensitivity analysis demonstrates that increasing maximum agent count $\gamma$ from 2 to 6 boosts HumanEval pass@1 from 88.50% to 90.62% (Figure 5, p. 8). Increasing beyond 6 to 10 agents yields marginal performance gain while inflating per-query inference cost by 1.5× (Section 5.5, p. 8).
- Role → model placement:
  * Ablation confirms role-to-model routing is the single most critical factor: Table 3 (p. 8) shows removing the LLM router $F_\theta^m$ results in the largest performance collapse (-2.09% on GSM8K, -4.34% on MATH), far more damaging than removing mode selection (-1.61% and -2.65%) or role allocation (-0.75% and -2.41%).
  * Domain-aligned heterogeneous assignment: MasRouter routes math analysis to instruction-tuned math models (e.g., Qwen-math), verification/testing to strict instruction-following models, and coding to code-specialized models (Figure 2, p. 4; Section 4.3).
  * Plug-and-play injection into homogeneous MAS: Converting homogeneous MAS (MAD, MacNet) into heterogeneous routed teams raises performance by +0.36% to +1.55% while reducing inference costs by 17.21% to 28.17% on HumanEval, and saving $6.17–$7.63 on MMLU (Table 2, p. 7).
- Verification / review effects: Role allocation sequentially couples generation with validation: for coding, programmer roles are progressively coupled to test engineer roles ($F_\theta^r$, Section 4.2, p. 5), preventing syntax and logic errors before final answer synthesis.
- Cost / tokens / latency: MasRouter slashes HumanEval cost from $0.363 to $0.185 (52.07% cost reduction) compared to SOTA baselines (Abstract, p. 1; Section 1, p. 3). On MBPP, it reduces inference overhead by 40.22%–43.78% while improving accuracy by 1.8%–8.0% over AFlow (Section 5.2, p. 7). Training resource cost is 69.57% lower than GPTSwarm and 83.51% lower than AFlow on MMLU (Table 12; Section 5.2, p. 7).
- Failure modes observed: (1) Single-agent routers fail on MAS because they are topology-unaware and cannot coordinate multi-step role dependencies; (2) Unconstrained multi-agent debate (MAD) causes severe token blowups without accuracy gains unless restricted by cost penalty $\lambda$; (3) Removing cost evaluation $C(\cdot)$ inflates token overhead by 54.09% and 41.62% (Section 5.5, p. 8).

## Implications for Hannibal's Army (label each as INFERENCE)
- Depth-1 design: INFERENCE: In single-tier delegation (Orchestrator → Workers), worker role definition must precede model assignment; workers should not be given generic prompts with the same model, but typed roles (e.g. test engineer vs implementer) matched to cost-effective specialized models.
- Depth-2 design: INFERENCE: For two-tier hierarchy (Field Marshal → Generals → Soldiers), the Field Marshal must act as $F_\theta^t$ and $F_\theta^r$ (determining the collaborative structure and assigning discrete worker roles), while Generals ensure soldiers adhere to domain-specific routing without expanding width beyond 4–6 agents per sub-orchestrator.
- Which agent/model type goes in which seat:
  * Orchestrator / Root Coordinator: High-context semantic encoder / frontier reasoner that assesses problem difficulty and selects collaboration mode.
  * Specialized Sub-tasks (Math/Coding/Testing): Heterogeneous domain-specialized models (e.g., cheap code/math specialists or Gemini Flash) rather than a single monolithic frontier model across all seats.
  * Verifiers / Reviewers: Fast, strict validation models paired downstream of generative implementers.
  * Cost constraint: Always enforce an explicit cost penalty parameter $\lambda$ or token budget gate in the orchestrator to prevent 1.5× cost explosions from unneeded agent proliferation.

## Validity
- Evidence grade: A (Peer-reviewed long paper, accepted at ACL 2025; extensive evaluation across 5 benchmarks, 20 baselines, ablation studies, and sensitivity analysis).
- Threats: Evaluated on short-form benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, MATH, HumanEval, MBPP) rather than massive multi-file repositories; maximum agent count investigated was capped at $\gamma \le 10$.
