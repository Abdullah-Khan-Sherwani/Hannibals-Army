**INFERENCE — staged evaluation:**

1. Mine existing campaign logs; preregister acceptance and provider budgets. Count stalls, rotations, rejected reviews and interventions.
2. **12 runs:** one greenfield system plus one major multi-module update × three arms (serial owner; owner+scouts; owner+scouts+builders) × two repetitions. Freeze starts/acceptance; randomize order. Include all preparation costs.
3. **8 runs:** two held-out campaigns × winning arm versus serial × two repetitions. Promote only with identical acceptance, no observed critical regression/violation, and ≥20% lower elapsed time or API-equivalent spend without more human effort.

Kill on budget exhaustion, critical defects or ownership violations; otherwise require the same promotion gate. Stop before this stage if shallow delegation fails. Maximum **28 runs**, screening rather than population-level proof.

**INFERENCE — cost unit:** Per accepted campaign, record provider-specific allowance percentage points separately, tokens/API-equivalent dollars, elapsed minutes and human-intervention minutes.
